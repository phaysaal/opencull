CURRENT REVIEW

          
Diagnostic checks

          

          
        

      

      

        

          External drive unavailable
          
Reconnect the same volume, wait for Finder to show it, then retry. The report and human review remain intact.

        

        

          Interrupted culling job
          
Open Queue and choose Retry from checkpoint. OpenCull validates the checkpoint before continuing.

        

        

          Stale human review
          
The report changed after review. Open the matching report or keep this sidecar read-only; OpenCull will not silently remap decisions.

        

        

          Preview cannot decode
          
Use Retry preview on the photograph. RAW files remain untouched and the failure is isolated to the generated cache.
